AMCOL Advanced Ship Stability Simulator v1.15.3
Draft Survey Training Missions + Cleaner Control Panel

MAIN FEATURES
1. Added a complete Draft Survey Training Mission system in the Draft Survey workspace.
2. Added four built-in AMCOL FORTUNE missions:
   - Mission A · Full Bulk Loading (Foundation)
   - Mission B · Top-Off Loading (Intermediate)
   - Mission C · Full Discharge (Intermediate)
   - Mission D · Dock-Water Top-Off (Advanced density-correction case)
3. Missions issue separate Initial and Final raw observation sheets containing:
   - FWD Port / Starboard draughts
   - MID Port / Starboard draughts
   - AFT Port / Starboard draughts
   - observed water density
   - changing other/consumables deductible
   - ballast tank Sounding / Ullage readings with liquid density
4. The true cargo quantity is hidden until the student submits the mission.
5. The student must transfer the observations into the existing Draft Survey calculator, calculate tank ballast, complete the Initial and Final survey and report cargo loaded/discharged.
6. Assessment scoring is 100 points:
   - Draught observations: 20
   - Water density: 10
   - Ballast sounding/ullage: 20
   - Other changing deductibles: 10
   - Survey calculated cargo: 25
   - Student reported cargo: 15
7. Grades: Distinction / Very Good / Good / Satisfactory / Requires Review.
8. Added printable mission assessment with student name/ID, component scores and hidden ground-truth cargo after submission.
9. Tank-sheet copy helper is explicitly marked as ASSISTED PRACTICE in the assessment; independent manual entry remains available.
10. Mission ground truth is generated from the exact rounded observation/tank sheet issued to the student so grading is fair and reproducible.
