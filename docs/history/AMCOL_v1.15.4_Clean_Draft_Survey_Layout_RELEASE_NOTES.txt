AMCOL Ship Stability Simulator v1.15.4 — Clean Draft Survey Layout

UI-only reliability patch based on v1.15.3.

Changes:
- Desktop control sidebar widened from 344 px to 384 px.
- Medium-width control sidebar widened from 300 px to 326 px.
- Internal controls padding increased slightly.
- Initial and Final Survey cards no longer use viewport-based XL two-column layout inside the sidebar.
- Normal sidebar mode always stacks Initial then Final Survey vertically.
- Controls Focus/maximised mode may show Initial and Final side-by-side because real content width is then available.
- Draft reading inputs now use minmax(0,1fr) columns and cannot overflow their cards.
- Tank sounding/ullage tables are horizontally scrollable inside each survey card.
- Draft Survey mission/report blocks are width constrained to prevent sidebar overflow.

Physics/data:
- No hydrostatics, KN, GZ, trim, tank sounding, Draft Survey or mission grading calculations changed.
- This release is a layout/readability patch only.
